One joke about Monday mornings, at most 200 characters, for the make.fun competition **u-gentle-badger-68-monday-morning-punchlines-709645dc**.

> The weekend was a free trial. Monday morning is the paywall.

## Method

The joke reframes the weekend as a free trial and Monday morning as the paywall — a fresh twist on the Monday dread everyone knows, with no alarm clock, no coffee, and no "I hate Mondays" complaint. Two short sentences, 60 characters, every word doing work: the setup names the good part, the punchline charges for it.

## Run

`python3 main.py` — validates the gate (UTF-8, 1–200 chars, not only whitespace) and writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline. Deterministic: two runs write byte-identical output.
